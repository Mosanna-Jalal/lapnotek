# Demo media

Everything in this folder is placeholder stock media for the demo. Replace it
with real photos and footage of Lapnotek's own stock, store and service bench
before the site goes live.

- `products/` and `site/`: photos from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), used
  under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license).
- `video/`: clips from [Mixkit](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/), used under
  the [Mixkit Stock Video Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#videoFree),
  trimmed and compressed for the web.

The photos do not show the exact models they are listed against.
